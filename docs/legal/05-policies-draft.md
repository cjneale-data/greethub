# ACCEPTABLE USE / CONTENT POLICY

**DRAFT FOR ATTORNEY REVIEW** · Effective [EFFECTIVE DATE]

GreetHub makes cards and books for people you love. These rules keep that safe for everyone. Our AI and print partners have rules of their own, and a break of theirs is a break of ours.

**Not allowed, ever**
1. Any sexual or nude content, and any sexual or exploitative content involving a minor. We block it, we may report it to authorities as the law requires, and we close the account.
2. Intimate images of anyone made or shared without their consent, and deepfakes of real people intended to deceive or humiliate.
3. Hate, harassment, threats, or content meant to shame, bully or dox a person.
4. Violence or self-harm instructions, illegal activity, weapons, drugs, or scams.
5. Real public figures, politicians, celebrities, or content that falsely suggests someone said or endorsed something.
6. Characters, logos, brands or artwork that belong to someone else (for example cartoon or movie characters). Ask for "a friendly raccoon", not a named character.
7. Photos you don't have the right to use, or photos of other people without their permission. Photos of anyone under 18 only from their parent or legal guardian, or with that person's written permission.
8. Medical, legal, or financial advice presented as a gift card, or fake documents.
9. Attempts to get around our filters, reverse-engineer our system, scrape it, or use our outputs to train another AI model.
10. Using GreetHub to produce spam, mass-produced low-quality books for resale, or anything for commercial resale without our written agreement.

**What happens if the rules are broken:** we may decline the job, delete the content, cancel the order, close the account, and report to authorities where required. Sympathy and get-well cards that mention death or illness are welcome; cruelty is not.
