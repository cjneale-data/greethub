## How to read this

Every fact carries a source URL and the date I read it (all 2026-10-11 unless shown). Tags: **[V]** I fetched the primary page (the fetch tool summarises the page, so exact wording should be re-checked before quoting in ads); **[S]** secondary source or search-result snippet; **[U]** unverified or my own arithmetic. Vendor "best of" blogs are competitor marketing and are tagged [S] with a bias flag. "Not found" means I looked and could not find it, not that it does not exist.

Limits of this pass: I could not use the Meta Ad Library, Google Keyword Planner, SimilarWeb, Semrush or Trustpilot's live pages, so traffic numbers are mostly absent. Several sites returned 404/403/certificate errors (Papier US cards page, Thoughtful, Punkpost, American Greetings, Mixbook). Those rows are thin and say so.

## Part A. Personalized children's books

### A1. Wonderbly (incl. Lost My Name) - the category leader
- **Sells:** 150+ personalized children's and adult titles, template stories with name and character-trait personalization, printed hardcover/softcover. [S] https://global.penguinrandomhouse.com/announcements/prh-acquires-wonderbly-one-of-the-uks-fastest-growing-independent-publishers-and-leader-in-personalized-gift-books/ (search snippet, read 2026-10-11)
- **Price:** Home page "from" prices $29.99 to $39.99 on the four featured titles [V] https://www.wonderbly.com/ . A third-party guide lists 8x10 hardcover $44.99, layflat $64.99, softcover $34.99 [S] https://greenlitcontent.com/resources/wonderbly-review . Not found on a primary price page for full range.
- **Shipping:** Primary delivery page returned 404. Secondary: US flat $5, 5-8 business days (one source) and 11 days order-to-mailbox (2026 review) [S] https://wetried.it/wonderbly-review/ . Treat as 8-14 days; confirm at a real checkout.
- **Promotions:** BOGO50 (second book half price) and $5 off first order for newsletter [V] https://www.wonderbly.com/ .
- **Personalization depth:** Name, appearance traits, dedication message printed on first page [V]. No photo-to-illustration. Trustpilot summary's main complaint: limited character appearance options [S].
- **AI use:** Hand-drawn art; no photo AI [S] https://skazka.ai/us/blog/best-personalized-childrens-books-2026 (competitor blog, biased).
- **Ratings:** Trustpilot 4.5 of 5 on about 84,000 company reviews [S] https://uk.trustpilot.com/review/wonderbly.com?page=6 . Praise: print quality, personalization. Complaints: late or never-arrived deliveries, especially in holiday season; company replies point to support [S] https://www.trustpilot.com/review/wonderbly.com/product/-69e1ccb0682069ef96f55e88 .
- **Scale:** Acquired by Penguin Random House, announced 2025-06-04, price undisclosed; 11M+ books sold, 140+ countries [S] https://www.shelf-awareness.com/theshelf/2025-06-05/penguin_random_house_acquires_wonderbly.html . Revenue estimates from aggregators range $3M to $100M and are useless [U] (https://getlatka.com/companies/aspire-it-services/vs/wonderbly , read 2026-10-11).
- **Channels:** Not directly observed. Heavy affiliate/coupon-site presence (Australia and UK coupon pages in results) and gifting editorial [S].
- **Strengths:** Brand, catalogue, publisher backing, print quality reputation, retail/gift credibility. **Weaknesses:** Child does not look like the child; delivery-delay complaints at peak.

### A2. Hooray Heroes
- **Sells:** Books for 1-4 children, siblings, dads, moms, grandparents, couples, pets, pet memorial, Christmas; plush add-ons. States books are made "without AI, by human designers and writers"; 3M+ books sold since 2013; US printing. [V] https://www.hoorayheroes.com/
- **Price:** Many books $59.99 list, shown at $39.99; one at $49.99 list, $29.99 now. Tax and shipping at checkout. Shipping cost/time not found. [V] same URL.
- **Personalization:** Character builder (skin, hair, eyes), story order choice, dedication. No photo AI.
- **Ratings:** Trustpilot about 4.3 on about 7,500 reviews [S] https://www.trustpilot.com/review/hoorayheroes.com/product/the-fun-tastic-five-6a04c603dc49fee91862a75c .
- **Signal:** Home page says some books are temporarily unavailable during a move to a new website [V]. Watch for pricing and catalogue changes next month.
- **Strengths:** Family-member books (grandparents, couples), "no AI" positioning. **Weaknesses:** discount-dependent list price, no likeness.

### A3. I See Me
- **Sells:** Name-based and photo books, board books, coloring books, pets, hand-drawn portraits, puzzles, ornaments. Featured $29.99 to $34.99. Promo EARLY25 (25% off), US site only, expires 2026-10-31. [V] https://www.iseeme.com/
- **Ratings:** 4.7 stars on Google Reviews, 1,970 reviews (as displayed by the site) [V] same URL.
- **Shipping:** page not found (404); not found.
- **Personalization:** name plus real-time preview; no photo AI [S].
- **Strengths:** Low price, classroom/baby catalogue, long history. **Weakness:** template illustrations.

### A4. Imagitime - the closest direct analogue (photo-based, 8.5x8.5, AI-assisted, US print)
- **Sells:** Custom book made from the child's photo(s), 8.5x8.5, paperback or hardcover; whole-book preview in about 2 minutes before ordering. [V] https://www.imagitime.com/
- **Price (US):** Paperback $39.99, hardcover $54.99, shipping $9.99, 10% off for email subscribers. Delivery US 8-13 days. [V] same URL.
- **Privacy claims:** photos not used for AI training or promotions (claimed); unsaved previews erased after 24 hours; saved previews and order images kept 12 months. [V] same URL. (GreetHub's 90-day purge is stricter.)
- **Ratings/scale:** Trustpilot about 4.8 on 3,600-4,000 reviews; site claims 300,000+ customers (self-reported) [S] https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/imagitime.com?page=5 .
- **AI use:** Told Wired its books are "not simply AI-generated" but edited by a creative team of writers and illustrators [S] https://aiweekly.co/alerts/parents-push-back-on-ai-picture-books-from-grandparents (summary of Wired, secondary of secondary).
- **Strengths:** Same format and print spec as GreetHub, instant full preview, strong reviews. **Weaknesses:** $64.98 all-in for hardcover; named in the 2026 "grandparents gifting AI slop" coverage.

## Part B. AI picture-book and storybook generators

- **Google Gemini Storybook** (launched about Aug 2025): describe a story, optionally upload photos or drawings, get a 10-page illustrated book with read-aloud narration in 45+ languages; print from browser; available to users 18+; no price listed on page (free with Gemini per press). No print-ordering service found. [V] https://gemini.google/overview/storybook/ ; [S] https://www.droid-life.com/2025/08/05/gemini-storybook/ . **This is the zero-price substitute and the main DIY threat**: a vendor blog rates its consistency as "weak; varies page to page" (biased) [S] https://little-hero.app/en/guides/ai-childrens-books-compared-2026 .
- **ChatGPT / Midjourney DIY:** consistency is "the hardest problem, each image generated independently", no print pipeline, manual assembly (same biased source). Not independently tested by me.
- **Subscription generators** (ToonyStory, Childbook.ai, StoryWonderBook): see A5.
- **Review/sentiment:** Parent backlash is now a named story. Futurism (2026-08-04, summarising Wired) reports grandparents gifting AI books; a dad said his 7- and 3-year-old were "bored" by them; some parents refuse any child photo going to AI; researcher warned families do not know how photos are stored. [V] https://futurism.com/artificial-intelligence/parents-horrified-ai-children-book . NC State study (13 parent-child pairs, published 2025-11-12): most parents accept AI images if text is human-authored and experts reviewed the images; most uncomfortable with AI-written text; prefer a cover label saying whether AI illustrated the book. [V] https://news.ncsu.edu/?p=1531242 . Small sample.

## Part D. Adjacent substitutes

- **Photo books (Shutterfly, Mixbook, Artifact Uprising, Snapfish):** you do layout; real photos, not illustrated story. Shutterfly BOGO photo books and up to 50% off running now [V] https://www.shutterfly.com/greeting-cards/ . They set the discount expectation.
- **AI image apps and DIY (ChatGPT, Gemini, Midjourney):** free or cheap; no print pipeline; consistency and assembly burden on the user [S].
- **Gift sites with custom products (Etsy, Zazzle, Minted):** price anchors above.
- **Pet and memorial books** (Hooray Heroes) show category adjacency [V].

## What the map says (feeds file 01)

1. The closest competitor by format, price band and promise is **Imagitime**. By technology and price pressure it is **ToonyStory / Magic Story / Childbook.ai**. By DIY threat it is **Gemini Storybook**.
2. In cards the closest analogue is **Cardwise** (AI cards, saved relationships, $6.99 mailed), with **Hallmark** and **Moonpig** as the incumbents setting the free-postage norm.
3. No competitor found offers both cards and books with one saved set of characters. Absence of evidence only; recheck monthly.
