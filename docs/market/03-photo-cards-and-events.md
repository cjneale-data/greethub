# Photo cards and events: competitor teardown

Prepared by: Market Intelligence, wave 1 of the photo cards and events project. Read date for every source below: 2026-10-15 unless stated. Tags: [V] primary page fetched and read, [S] secondary or search snippet, [U] unverified or own arithmetic. "Not found" means I looked and could not confirm it. Builds on docs/products/photo-cards-and-events-plan.md sections 1-2. Not legal advice.

**Honest limit of this teardown.** Several primary pages blocked the fetch (Minted 403, Canva 403, Evite pricing 404, Artifact Uprising category 404) or loaded without prices. Prices at exactly 25, 50 and 100 are rare on any public page; most vendors show a per-card "from" price that falls with quantity. Where a number is a single quantity, I say which. Most prices are mid-promotion (Oct 2026 sales), so list versus sale matters. Do not quote any figure here in customer-facing copy.

## 2. Per competitor

**Minted.** Direct page fetch failed (403). Evidence is thin and old: $2.28 each at 50 basic [Penny Hoarder, updated 2022-11-21, https://www.thepennyhoarder.com/save-money/holiday-greeting-cards/ , S]; minimum order 25 and free recipient addressing as pros [Top Ten Reviews, https://www.toptenreviews.com/photo-cards-minted-review , S]; 2021 holiday websites by QR code, free with a card purchase [Business Wire, https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20211115005153/en , S]; one blog says free addressing was once limited-time, so confirm it is permanent. Promo: 15% off sitewide and 25% off save the dates, expired Sep 8, 2026 [coupon page via search, S]. The plan cites a Nov 2025 Business Wire release for free addressing; that page 403'd and I could not find it in search, so [U].

**Shutterfly.** Category page https://www.shutterfly.com/cards-stationery/holiday-cards [V]: flat, folded, gate fold and tri-fold; sizes 4x8, 5x7, 6x8; list $1.35 to $7.27 with sale $0.81 to $3.63 per card (labels unclear). Live promos: "10 free cards or 20 free when you buy 100+" (code FREECARDS), "up to 50% off almost everything", free shipping $99+, all ending Oct 18. Mail For Me and address printing prices from https://www.shutterfly.com/ideas/how-to-order-business-holiday-cards-in-bulk/ (page "Last Updated Aug 13, 2026") [V], business-oriented, "actual pricing varies". This is the only competitor with a confirmed, priced mail-for-me. Cost to a family at 50: 50 x $1.49 = $74.50 on top of cards [U, arithmetic]. Shutterfly's AI-card guide: https://www.shutterfly.com/ideas/how-to-make-a-christmas-card-with-ai-that-still-feels-personal/ [S, snippet only].

## 4. Gaps GreetHub can honestly take

1. **Proof checks on the words.** No competitor in what I read advertises weekday-vs-date, name-consistency or year checks. [U: absence of evidence on pages I could read; Minted, Canva and Paperless Post pages were blocked or not read.] Confirm with a live walkthrough of each editor before using this in copy.
2. **One design, paper and page.** Zola does paper plus free RSVP site for weddings; Minted offers holiday websites. Nobody found covers holiday cards, save the dates, invitations and parties with one design and one guest list. Zola is the real rival for weddings, and it is free.
3. **Mail for me for families.** Only Shutterfly prices it ($1.49 domestic, plain white envelopes only). Minted, Artifact Uprising, Zazzle: not found. Opportunity if the printer can do it at a similar price; threat if Minted matches.
4. **Addressing is table stakes.** Minted free, Zola free, Zazzle "free guest addressing", Shutterfly $0.42, Artifact Uprising $0.40. Phase 1 launching without printed addresses is a visible gap. Matches the CMO's note.
5. **Digital pricing.** Paperless Post is per recipient and rising; Evite jumps by guest tier. A flat $14.99 Event Plus is simpler, but Zola's free RSVP caps what couples will pay for weddings. Best target is non-wedding parties and holiday gatherings, and wedding hosts who want paper and page together.
6. **Promo fog.** Shutterfly "up to 50% off", Zazzle "up to 40%", Minted codes. A single plain price is a real differentiator, but also means GreetHub looks higher than sale prices on a Tuesday in October.
7. **AI help.** Nobody found with photo ranking or a letter writer, but Shutterfly's AI guide and "Ask Paige" mean parity is near. AI is a weak lead claim; the CMO already avoids it for Phase 1.

## 5. Threats

- Minted's free addressing plus designer templates is the premium benchmark; we cannot match design depth in Phase 1.
- Zola gives couples free RSVP, free addressing, a free site and cheap save the dates; it is hard to beat on price for weddings.
- Shutterfly and Zazzle run deep promos in October; a November launch at full price will look high.
