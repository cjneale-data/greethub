Prepared by: Head of Market Intelligence. Period: 2026-10-01 to 2026-10-11 plus events from the prior 16 months that set the baseline. Read date for all sources: 2026-10-11. Tags: [V] primary page read, [S] secondary, [U] unverified. This is the first report, so "what changed" lists dated events found rather than month-over-month deltas. Next report: 2026-11-10.

## 1. Executive summary

1. The closest direct competitor is **Imagitime**: same 8.5x8.5 format, photo-based, whole-book preview in about 2 minutes, $54.99 hardcover + $9.99 shipping, 8-13 days, about 4,000 Trustpilot reviews at 4.8 [V]/[S]. GreetHub's 24pp hardcover is $44.99 all-in, about $20 less.
2. **Public sentiment toward AI personalised books turned negative in summer 2026**: Wired (late July) and Futurism (2026-08-04) describe grandparents gifting AI books and parents objecting on privacy and quality grounds [V Futurism]. Imagitime was named. This lands directly on GreetHub's grandparent segment.
3. **Google Gemini Storybook** (free, 10 pages, photo inspiration, print from browser, 18+) remains the zero-price substitute; no print-ordering service found [V].
4. In cards, incumbents mail free (Hallmark Sign & Send, Moonpig free shipping) and AI card apps (Cardwise $6.99 mailed) have appeared. GreetHub's $10.98 mailed card and $19.99 digital book are out of line (02).
5. Q4 promotion season has started: Shutterfly 50% off cards (ends today), I See Me EARLY25 (to 2026-10-31), Wonderbly BOGO50, Moonpig 40% sign-up offer [V].
6. Benchmarks suggest the CMO's Google CPC is about 2.6x low; paid CAC is likely well above the $76-$89 modelled.
7. Confidence: moderate on prices (primary pages), low on traffic and ad data (no tools).

## 6. Opportunities

1. **Price gap to Imagitime** ($20 all-in on the same format), if likeness quality is shown.
2. **Privacy contrast** using our own facts (90-day purge vs 12-month retention at Imagitime [V]).
3. **Holiday delivery reliability** is Wonderbly's main complaint theme; an honest, early cut-off date is a differentiator.
4. **Saved People across cards and books**: nobody found offers it; Cardwise is the nearest.
5. **Backlash-aware positioning**: story from the customer's own details, AI-illustrated label on the cover (NC State preference [V]).

## 7. Threats

1. Gemini Storybook adds print ordering or deeper photo likeness (free, 18+).
2. Imagitime or Wonderbly (PRH-backed) cuts price or adds instant AI previews; PRH scale.
3. Continuing "AI slop" coverage depresses grandparent/parent purchase intent.
4. Q4 promotion intensity (25-50% codes) vs our flat price.
5. Cardwise-type entrants extend from cards into books.
6. Legal/vendor-term exposure on child photos (internal, `docs/legal/00-summary.md`).
