**Flags:**
1. **Postage line.** The $2.99 charge is the only place in the card market where a buyer sees a separate postage fee of that size. Incumbents mail free (Hallmark) or bundle it (Moonpig, Cardwise Gold, Escargot). Real postage is roughly 82c at volume (Postable's example [V]); the CFO's own note says $2.99 "covers about 2x real shipping." Present as one price.
2. **$10.98 all-in** is $3-$6 above Hallmark/Moonpig/Cardwise/Escargot. The justification (illustrated from your story, likeness) must be visible in the product. Suggested test: $8.99 postage-included (about $2 lower contribution; the CFO should model it; card contribution is already only $2.9-$4.1 per the CFO).
3. **Digital card $3.99** competes with free eCards (Hallmark+ unlimited, American Greetings) and Cardwise's free tier (5 AI cards, unlimited digital) [V]. Expect low conversion.

### Promotion patterns observed (2026-10-11)
- Wonderbly: BOGO50 and $5 off first order [V]. I See Me: EARLY25, 25% off, US only, ends 2026-10-31 [V]. Hooray Heroes: list $59.99 shown at $39.99 [V]. Imagitime: 10% email [V]. Shutterfly: 50% off all cards (ends 2026-10-11), BOGO photo books, SHIP99 [V]. Moonpig: 40% sign-up prompt [V]. Postable: 20% off with EARLY [V].
- **Pattern:** the industry runs near-permanent promotions with a list-price anchor. A flat $39 with no sale is normal-looking next to $39.99-shown-on-sale, but buyers will compare to codes. Plan a first-order incentive (CFO to price) and avoid fake "was" prices (FTC and `docs/legal` req 13).

### Conversion
- Site-wide e-commerce conversion: 2.26% (IRP Commerce, July 2026) to 2.72% (Dynamic Yield global); Shopify average 1.4%, top 20% 3.2%+, top 10% 4.7%+; add-to-cart about 6% (Dynamic Yield) or 4.6% (Littledata); checkout completion about 45%; cart abandonment 70-78% [S] https://blendcommerce.com/blogs/shopify/ecommerce-conversion-rate-benchmarks-2026 ; https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/ecommerce-benchmarks.md (snippets). Gift-specific figures: not found.
- CMO funnel for Google: visitor to sign-up 9% x sign-up to purchase 20% = 1.8% visitor-to-purchase. That is inside the benchmark band for a **warm, high-intent** audience but optimistic for a new, unreviewed brand selling a $45 product that requires a long form, photo upload and two consent boxes. For Meta: 0.7% visitor-to-purchase (cold), plausible.
- Photo-upload funnels carry extra friction: Imagitime's preview takes about 2 minutes [V]; ours takes 5-15 minutes for pages (CX). Expect lower conversion than the benchmarks until the speed is addressed.

### Specific suggestions for the CFO model
1. Raise Google CPC base to about $3.50-$4.50 (low $2.50, high $5.50) until Keyword Planner data replaces it. This lifts Google CAC to $150-$250 and further weakens paid payback.
2. Keep Meta CPC but cut visitor-to-signup/purchase by 20-30% in the Low case for the trust gap.
3. Blended contribution about $15.20 against any plausible paid CAC means paid acquisition does not pay back in the first order; model **first-order payback only for organic/referral** and treat paid as a test budget. The CFO's own finding (break-even month 7 with no paid) is consistent with this.
4. Model the card price change (postage included, $8.99 test) and digital book price as scenarios; digital volumes in the model should be low.
5. Add a first-order discount line (competitors run 10-25% codes; $5 off at Wonderbly).
6. Print cost: verify Lulu pricing and 24pp availability. I could not find a published Lulu price for 8.5x8.5 casewrap 24pp (Lulu's API is free; costs per job are print cost + shipping + fulfilment fee) [S] https://help.api.lulu.com/en/support/solutions/articles/64000254631-are-there-fees-to-use-lulu-s-print-api- . A forum snippet claims a 40-page minimum for hardcover [U]. If true the 24pp SKU does not exist and the price ladder changes.
7. Add a competitor-parity check each month (files 03/04): Imagitime $54.99/$9.99 and Wonderbly shipping are the two inputs most likely to move your price ceiling.
