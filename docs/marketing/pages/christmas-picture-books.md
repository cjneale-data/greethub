# A Christmas picture book starring your family

Turn one December memory, or an invented adventure, into a hardcover picture book your family can read every year. You tell us the story and add a few photos, and we draw everyone once for you to approve.

## How it works

1. **Tell us the story.** A real memory or an invented adventure. A few sentences is enough.
2. **Add the people.** One to four people and a pet, with photos if you want a likeness. You approve how everyone is drawn before any page is.
3. **Approve and print.** We draw each page from the approved character sheet, you check the proof, and the book is printed to order.

## Story ideas for a Christmas book

You don't need to be a writer. Start with a situation and a feeling.

- **The year something changed.** The first Christmas in a new house, with a new baby, or without someone who used to carry the star to the top of the tree.
- **A trip to Grandma's.** The snowy drive, the smell of the kitchen, the game everyone cheats at. Specifics make the story.
- **A silly family legend.** The year the tree fell over, the dog and the ham, the sleigh made of cardboard. Funny stories are the ones kids ask for again.
- **An invented adventure.** Your child helping a small lantern-carrying fox find the way home through the snow. Original characters, drawn just for your book.
- **A thank-you in story form.** A book for a grandparent where the grandchildren are the heroes and Grandma is the one who always has the answer.

## Who it's for

**Grandparents.** A book where they appear, drawn kindly, is often kept long after other presents are put away.

**Children.** Kids tend to like a story that is about them and uses real names, pets and places. Read it aloud together.

**A whole family.** One book can include up to four people and a pet, so a small household can all be in it.

**Someone far away.** You can have it sent to one recipient. Sending to many recipients in one order isn't something we offer yet.

## How GreetHub makes it personal

The story comes from your words. We help shape it, but we don't add a generic plot with your child's name dropped in. Before drawing any pages, we draw each character once and show you the sheet. If something is off, you change it before the book is made, not after.

Photos are used to draw the people you add. Add only people you have the right to include, and only photos of children you are the parent or guardian of, or have permission for. You can read how photos are handled on our privacy and help pages.

The book is 8.5 by 8.5 inches, hardcover, in 24 or 32 pages. Characters are invented illustrations in the style you pick, not copies of any existing character.

## Templates that fit

- **our-summer, "Our Summer"** (book, adventurous): a rowboat on a lake at golden hour. Best if your story is a memory of time together; the setting changes to match your words.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to be able to write?**
No. A few sentences about the memory or the adventure is enough. The Greet Guide asks questions and helps you shape the story.

**Will the characters look like us?**
You add photos and a short description, and we draw the people as illustrated characters. You see them and approve them before any page is drawn.

**Can I change a page I don't like?**
Account holders can ask for page redraws on a book. You see the proof before you order.

**What happens to the photos I upload?**
They're used to draw your book. See our privacy page for retention and deletion details. If you have a question the page doesn't answer, ask us before you upload.

**Will it arrive by Christmas?**
We don't promise dates on this page. Books are printed to order, so timing depends on the print shop. Look for the order-by notice in the creator before you start.
