# A Valentine's card that sounds like you

Say it in your own words, with a picture drawn for the person who'll read it. It can be for a partner, but Valentine's cards for kids, friends, parents and grandparents are welcome too.

## How it works

1. **Tell us.** Chat with the Greet Guide or fill in a short form: who it's for, what you want to say, how it should feel.
2. **Check the look.** See a preview before anything is final, and change what's off.
3. **Approve and print.** When you say yes, we print the 5 by 7 folded card on real paper.

## What to write in a Valentine's card

The cards that get kept are specific.

- **Reach for one detail.** "I love how you hum when you think nobody can hear" does more than "You mean the world to me."
- **Say what you notice.** The small habits, the way they make a room feel.
- **Mix warm with light.** A joke in the middle of a sincere note feels like you.
- **Be as long as it's true.** Two lines is fine. Don't pad.
- **Skip the rhyme unless you enjoy it.** A plain sentence in your own voice beats borrowed verse.

Starters: "The thing I'd miss most is..." / "Out of everything this year, my favorite was..." / "I wanted you to see this written down."

## Ideas by who it's for

**A partner or spouse.** Mention a shared memory, such as the first apartment or the long drive where the playlist went wrong. Keep it real rather than grand.

**Your kids.** A silly, loving card from a parent can be a tradition. Describe them as an invented character if you like, such as a small explorer, and we draw it.

**Friends.** Galentine's-style cards are a good fit. Funny and warm travels well.

**Parents and grandparents.** Valentine's is a gentle reason to say what you rarely say out loud.

**Yourself, or someone you're getting to know.** A card doesn't need to be a declaration. "I'm glad we met" is a good place to start.

## How GreetHub makes it personal

The front is drawn for this card in a style and mood you choose, such as soft and romantic, funny, or plain and calm. You see it before it's final. The message inside is yours, and we don't add stock verse.

You can have the card mailed to you to sign and send, or to one recipient. Mailing one card to many people in one order isn't something we offer yet.

For a longer keepsake, a [personalized picture book](/create/book) can tell a shared story, such as how you met or a trip you took. We draw invented, illustrated characters and never lookalikes of real people or existing characters.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is it only for couples?**
No. A Valentine's card can be for anyone you love. Pick the person and the tone in the creator.

**Can I write my own message?**
Yes. Write every word, or let the Greet Guide help you draft and edit it.

**Can I see it before I pay?**
You can make one watermarked preview as a guest, with no account. A free account lets you keep it and see a full-size proof before you order.

**Can I put a funny message on a romantic card?**
Yes. Choose a funny tone, or write your own line. The art follows the mood you set.

**Will it arrive by February 14?**
We don't promise dates on this page. Check the order-by notice in the creator, and a digital card is an option if you're short on time.
