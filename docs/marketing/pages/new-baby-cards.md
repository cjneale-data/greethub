# New baby cards that say more than congratulations

A new baby brings a lot of cards, and most say the same few words. A card made from what you know about this family, and what you hope for this child, is the one that gets kept in the memory box.

## How it works

1. **Tell us about the baby and the family.** A name, if they have chosen one, and a few things about the new parents or siblings.
2. **Choose your tone.** Soft and heartfelt, or light and funny for tired parents.
3. **Preview, then order.** You see the card before it prints and can edit the words.

## What to write in a new baby card

New parents are tired and overwhelmed. They do not need advice. They need to feel seen. A few ways in:

- **Welcome the baby by name.** "Welcome to the world, Theo. Your family has been waiting for you."
- **Say something to the parents.** "You are going to be so good at this, and you are allowed to be bad at it for a while."
- **Offer something real.** "No need to write back. I will drop off dinner Thursday." Only write this if you mean it.
- **Share a wish for the child.** "May you always be curious and may you always be loved."
- **Keep it kind to every kind of family.** Adoptions, surrogacy, two moms, two dads, single parents, and grandparents raising a grandchild all deserve to hear "congratulations." Use the words the family uses.

Avoid comments about the baby's looks, sleep, weight, or how the parents "should" feel. If you are not sure what to say, short is safe.

## Ideas to make it personal

- **A card for the sibling.** An older brother or sister going through a big change may love a card of their own, about being a big sibling.
- **A bedtime story.** A picture book of 24 or 32 pages can tell the baby's arrival as a story, with the family as characters. It may become a favorite for the first few years.
- **A letter for later.** Write a message the child can read when they are older, and tell us it is meant for them.
- **A note from the grandparents.** Ask them for a line, or a memory of the parents as babies.
- **Something for the first-time dad or non-birthing parent.** They get fewer cards. A short, honest one can mean a lot.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I make a card before the baby arrives?**
Yes. You can write a card of hopes and wishes before the birth, and many people do. You can also wait to use the name.

**What if I do not know the baby's name yet?**
Write it to "the new baby" or to the parents. You can add the name later when you are ready to order.

**Is this the right gift for a baby shower?**
It can be. A card works as a gift tag, and a picture book works as a gift. Tell us who it is from.

**Do I need to upload a photo?**
No. Photos are optional.

**Can I write in another tone, such as funny?**
Yes. A funny card for sleepless parents is often well received. Choose a tone when you start.
