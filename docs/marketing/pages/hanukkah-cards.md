# A Hanukkah card with your own words

Write a Hanukkah card that sounds like you, with a picture drawn for the person who'll open it. Tell us who it's for and what you want to say, and we take it from there.

## How it works

1. **Tell us.** Chat with the Greet Guide or fill in a short form: who it's for, what you want to say, what feeling you're after.
2. **Check the look.** See a preview before anything is final, and change what's off.
3. **Approve and print.** When you say yes, we print the 5 by 7 folded card on real paper.

## What to write in a Hanukkah card

A good Hanukkah message is short, warm and about the person.

- **Start with the greeting you'd really use.** "Happy Hanukkah" or "Chag Sameach" are both fine. Use whichever is natural for you and for them.
- **Say something specific.** A memory of making latkes together, a candle-lighting you remember, or how glad you are that the family will be around the table.
- **Think light.** The holiday is about light in a dark season, so notes about hope, warmth and getting through a hard year fit well.
- **Mention the future.** "See you for the second night?" gives the card a next step.

If you're not sure about wording for a family whose observance differs from yours, keep it simple and kind. The Greet Guide can help you shape a short message, and you review every word.

## Ideas by who it's for

**Grandparents.** Mention a tradition they started, such as the way they hand out the gelt, and thank them for it.

**Friends and neighbours who celebrate.** A brief, warm message that names them and wishes them a bright eight nights.

**Interfaith families.** A card that speaks to the whole household, in your own words, without leaning on one set of traditions.

**Kids.** Funny and light often works best: a card about the dreidel that never lands the way they want.

**Teachers and colleagues.** A short thank-you for the year, with a Hanukkah greeting if you know they celebrate.

## How GreetHub makes it personal

The front is drawn for this card, not pulled from a stock set. You choose the feeling (warm, funny, quiet) and a style, and you see the result before you commit. The message inside is yours. We don't add stock verse.

You can have the card mailed to you to sign and send, or mailed straight to one recipient. Sending to a long list in one order isn't something we offer yet.

We draw invented, illustrated people. We won't make lookalikes of real people or of existing characters.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I ask for a menorah, dreidels or other Hanukkah scenes?**
Yes, describe what you'd like. Check the preview carefully, since AI-drawn symbols can come out wrong. You can ask for a redraw before you approve.

**Can I write in Hebrew?**
We haven't tested Hebrew in the card text, so we can't promise it prints correctly. You can always write the greeting in English and add a line of your own by hand.

**Can I see it before I pay?**
You can make one watermarked preview as a guest, with no account. A free account lets you keep it and see a full-size proof before you order.

**Can it be mailed to the person directly?**
Yes, to one recipient per order. Sending to many recipients at once isn't available yet.

**Will it arrive for the first night?**
We don't promise delivery dates on this page. Hanukkah moves each year, so check the order-by notice in the creator.
