# A Mother's Day card with your words and her picture

Make a Mother's Day card that says something only you could say. Tell us about her and what you'd like her to know, and we draw the front for her.

## How it works

1. **Tell us.** Chat with the Greet Guide or fill in a short form: who it's for, what you want to say, how it should feel.
2. **Check the look.** See a preview before anything is final, and change what's off.
3. **Approve and print.** When you say yes, we print the 5 by 7 folded card on real paper.

## What to write in a Mother's Day card

Mothers hear "thanks for everything" a lot. They keep the cards that notice something.

- **Name something she taught you.** "You taught me to check the sky before deciding anything" is hers and nobody else's.
- **Say what you only see now.** Many people understand their mothers differently as adults. Say so.
- **Use her real names.** Mom, Mum, Mama, Nana. Whatever you call her, write it.
- **Keep it truthful.** A relationship can be complicated and a card can still be kind. Choose the true things you're glad of.
- **Add one line for today.** "I'm thinking of you this morning" is enough.

Starters: "I was remembering the time you..." / "Something I never said..." / "If I could give you one thing it would be..."

## Ideas by who it's for

**Your mom.** Pick a specific memory and describe it. A hand-drawn scene of something you did together is more personal than a generic bouquet.

**Your grandmother.** Plain, warm wording with a family detail she'd know. Describe her kitchen, her garden or her chair.

**A mother figure.** Stepmothers, aunts, mentors, neighbours and family friends. Say what role they played. It matters to be named.

**A new mom.** Keep it gentle and short. Acknowledge the work and say you're proud of her.

**From the kids.** Little ones can tell you what they'd say, and you write it down in their words. Fun, honest lines are the ones she keeps. Describe the family as invented characters if you like.

**Someone missing her mother.** If Mother's Day is hard for her, a quiet note that says "I'm thinking of you today" is kind.

## How GreetHub makes it personal

The front is drawn for this card, in a mood and style you pick, from paper-cut and bold to soft and watercolor. You see it before it's final. The words inside are yours, and we don't add stock verse.

You can have the card mailed to you, or to one recipient. Mailing to many recipients in one order isn't something we offer yet.

If a card feels small for the occasion, a [personalized picture book](/create/book) can hold a longer story: a memory with her, drawn with the people you choose. You approve how everyone looks before any page is drawn. Characters are invented illustrations, not copies of anyone famous or any existing character.

## Templates that fit

- **mum-you-did-it, "Mom, You Did It"** (card, heartfelt, paper-cut collage): a layered paper flower and a small cut-paper teapot on plum. A good fit for a message of thanks, and warm rather than sugary.
- **for-nonna, "For Nonna"** (card, heartfelt, watercolor): a sunlit table with lemons, tea and wildflowers. It's built for a birthday, but the mood suits a grandmother on Mother's Day. Change the occasion in the chat.

For a gift that lasts, the picture book templates **our-summer** ("Our Summer") and **night-the-moon-came-down** ("The Night the Moon Came Down") are starting points, and your story replaces the scene.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I write my own message?**
Yes. Write every word, or let the Greet Guide help you draft and edit it.

**Can I add my kids or our family?**
Yes. Describe the people and we draw invented characters from your description. On a picture book you can add photos so the characters look like your family.

**Can I see it before I pay?**
You can make one watermarked preview as a guest, with no account. A free account lets you keep it and see a full-size proof before you order.

**Can it be mailed straight to her?**
Yes, to one recipient. Sending to many recipients at once isn't available yet.

**Will it arrive by Mother's Day?**
We don't promise dates on this page. Check the order-by notice in the creator, and a digital card is an option if time is short.
