# Birthday cards for Mom that sound like you

A store card says happy birthday in someone else's words. A GreetHub card turns what you actually remember about your mom into a short illustrated story, so she gets something only you could have written.

## How it works

1. **Tell us about her.** Answer a few friendly questions: her name, what she loves, a moment you still laugh about.
2. **Add a photo if you like.** A favorite picture helps us get the details right. You pick whether to use one.
3. **Check the preview, then order.** You see the card before anything is printed, and you can change the words.

## What to write in a birthday card for Mom

The best birthday messages are specific. "You are the best mom" is lovely but could go in anyone's card. These shapes tend to land:

- **Name one habit.** "You still answer the phone on the first ring, just in case it is one of us."
- **Say what she taught you.** "You taught me to always bring a snack and to apologize first."
- **Look back, then forward.** "Thank you for every ride to practice. I hope this year has a lot of sitting down in it."
- **Keep it short.** Three or four honest lines beat a page of general praise.

If you are stuck, start with "I still remember when..." and finish the sentence. You can type a rough version and we will help tidy it, but the memory has to come from you.

## Ideas to make it personal

- **A running joke.** The burnt pancakes of 1998, the family road-trip playlist, the way she narrates every movie. Funny cards work best when only your family gets the joke.
- **Her favorite things, drawn in.** Lemons and tea, a garden in bloom, a worn paperback. Tell us what she loves and we can work it into the illustration.
- **A message from everyone.** Siblings can each contribute a line to the brief, so the card reads like the whole family wrote it.
- **A card that is also a keepsake.** If a card feels too small for a milestone birthday, a picture book of 24 or 32 pages can tell a longer story about her.

## How GreetHub makes it personal

You bring the memories and a few details. GreetHub turns them into original illustrations and a message in the tone you choose, from warm to playful. Everything is made from your brief, and we use original characters, never anyone's famous cartoons. The illustrations are made with AI, and the words start from what you write. You review the result before it is printed, so nothing arrives as a surprise to you.

Cards are 5x7 and fold like a regular card. Picture books are 8.5x8.5 hardcover.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I write the message myself?**
Yes. Write as much or as little as you want. You can type your own words and we will use them, or give us a few memories and we will help shape them. You approve the final wording.

**Do I have to upload a photo?**
No. A photo is optional. If you add one, it is used to make your card and handled under our privacy terms, which are linked from the upload step.

**Can I see the card before it is printed?**
Yes. You see a preview first and can change it before you order.

**Can it be funny instead of sentimental?**
Yes. Choose a funny tone and share the jokes only your family knows. If you are unsure, a short heartfelt line with one funny detail is a safe mix.

**Can I make one for a stepmom, mother-in-law or someone who is like a mom?**
Yes. Just tell us who she is to you. The words and the story follow what you share.
