# Who are you celebrating?

Pick a person or an occasion, and we will point you to ideas, wording and a starting design. Every card and book is made from your own words, and you see a preview before anything is printed.

## How it works

1. **Choose an occasion below,** or start from scratch if yours is not listed.
2. **Tell us about the person.** A few questions, a few details, and a photo only if you want to.
3. **Preview, change what you like, then order.**

## Cards or books?

A **card** is a 5x7 folded card, quick to make and easy to send. A **picture book** is an 8.5x8.5 hardcover of 24 or 32 pages that tells a longer story. If you are unsure, start with a card.

[Make a card](/create/card) or [make a picture book](/create/book).

## How GreetHub makes it personal

You supply the memories, names and tone, and GreetHub turns them into original illustrations and words. The illustrations are made with AI and use original characters, never famous ones or lookalikes of real people. Photos are optional. You review the result before it prints.

Cards and books are made for each person, so there are no photo cards, event pages or mailing to a list of recipients in this first season. Those are not available yet.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need to upload a photo?**
No. Photos are optional. Many cards are made with words alone.

**Can I write the message myself?**
Yes. Write your own words, or share a few memories and we will help shape them. You approve the final text.

**Can I see it before it is printed?**
Yes. You see a preview first and can make changes.

**What if my occasion is not listed?**
Start from scratch, or browse the [template gallery](/browse). Birthdays, thank yous, get well, weddings and more are available.

**Is it made with AI?**
The illustrations are. The words start from what you tell us, and you decide what the card finally says.
