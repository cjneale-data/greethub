# GreetHub Contribution Policy (DRAFT for partners to sign)

Draft only. Not legal advice and not a contract until the owner's attorney has reviewed and adapted it and all partners have signed. Signing and any grant of equity are the owner's acts, not an agent's.

1. **Purpose.** Partners contribute to GreetHub before it can pay them. Their share is tracked by the dynamic method described in `01-model-spec.md` and is not fixed until a trigger event.
2. **Who earns.** Only the human partners named by the owner. AI tools never earn.
3. **Time.** Each partner logs real hours with date, category and note. A partner never logs time they did not work. Time is valued at that partner's recorded fair-market base rate, set once by the owner with the partner's agreement, and multiplied by 2. If the company pays cash for those hours, only the unpaid part counts. Work done by AI tools is not time; only the hours the partner personally spent count.
4. **Cash and expenses.** Money a partner spends on GreetHub and is not repaid is recorded with a receipt reference and multiplied by 4. Repaid amounts are removed.
5. **Other resources.** Equipment, supplies, IP, facilities valued at agreed fair value of use, multiplied by 2.
6. **Ideas.** Counted only through the idea ledger and rubric (`02-idea-rubric.md`), after adoption or shipping, within the caps.
7. **Approval.** An entry counts only after a different partner approves it. Disputes are resolved by the partners; the owner decides ties.
8. **Transparency.** Every partner can see the full ledger and current split at any time.
9. **Leaving.** The leaver table in `01-model-spec.md` section 6 applies. "Good reason" is defined as: [to be drafted by attorney].
10. **Trigger events.** On financing, profit distribution or sale, the split is frozen and converted under the company's legal documents: [to be drafted by attorney].
11. **No promise.** Slices are a record, not shares, wages or a promise of payment or ownership until converted by signed legal documents.
12. **Taxes.** Each partner is responsible for their own tax advice; the company will take advice on grants.
13. **Changes.** Multipliers, caps and rubric change only by written agreement of all partners and apply from that date forward.
