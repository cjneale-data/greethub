# 09. Open questions: owner decisions

Ordered by how soon they block work.

## Blocking P0 (answer before spending $35k)

1. **Do you want to fund P0 now, or wait for 6 months of online data?** Updated 2026-10-13: I now recommend waiting. Run S0 (about $100 to $500, no contractors) after launch and start P0 only when start gate S0 passes (07 section 2). $35k is about two thirds of the CFO's $54k peak funding need, and the app cannot carry a kiosk yet. Hardware build still waits for G0.
2. **Is a licence/partner route acceptable?** Selling or licensing the GreetHub software to an existing kiosk operator (Fujifilm/Scribbler-type, Card Isle-type) in exchange for placement, or do you want to own the hardware and the brand on the machine?
3. **How much cash will you commit to the whole program?** The base R&D to M36 is $1.1M; the realistic cumulative cash need including fleet capex is $1.8M or more. Is there a budget ceiling that should make P2 the end point?
4. **Which venue first?** Gift shop, campus bookstore, hospital, or grocery. Do you have personal access to a host? A warm introduction is worth more than any pitch.
5. **Price tolerance:** are you comfortable at $6.99 per kiosk card, below the online $7.99 + $2.99 postage? Should the kiosk hold the same price as online?

## Before P1

6. **Tier A/C first, Tier B later?** I recommend building the pilot from off-the-shelf parts and only engineering the freestanding machine after the field pilot proves demand. Agree?
7. **Photos at the kiosk:** the plan has none (no camera, saved People only). Scribbler says photo upload is 20% of its cards. Accept the trade-off for v1?
8. **Mail it later and stamps:** include mail-it-later (reuse the printer partners) in v1? Stamp sales are out of scope.
9. **Prerequisites:** are you willing to put the Supabase and security fixes (R1 to R5 in the assessment) ahead of the kiosk? The kiosk cannot launch without them.
10. **Languages:** English and Spanish for the pilot, or English only?
11. **Brand on the machine:** the GreetHub name is not trademark-cleared (`docs/legal/00-summary.md`). Is clearing it before a public wrap a precondition?

## Before P2 (field pilot)

12. **Host terms you will accept:** revenue share cap (I assumed 25% typical, 15% to 35% range), free placement, removal rights, exclusivity (recommend none).
13. **Who services the machines in the pilot?** You, a contractor, or the host's staff? The plan prices a contractor at about $100 a visit.
14. **Insurance and liability appetite** for a physical machine in a public place (property and general liability naming the host).
15. **Which states?** Each kiosk state means a sales-tax registration and possibly personal property tax; keep the pilot in the home state if possible.
16. **Pilot success thresholds:** are 5 cards/day, 15% account link and 97% print success the right gates, or do you want different numbers? Is "2 of 3 hosts continue" acceptable?

## Strategic

17. **Account acquisition value:** how much is a new GreetHub account worth to you compared with the paid CAC (about $70 in v3) or, with paid spend near $0, its repeat contribution (about $11 [U])? This one assumption can swing the kiosk from a distraction to a marketing channel.
18. **Online first or both?** If the online funnel needs the same engineering capacity (CTO, security, legal), what takes priority?
19. **Books at the kiosk:** the book flow needs photos and consent; the plan limits the kiosk to cards and a hand-off to the phone for books. Agree?
20. **Exit rule:** if G2 fails, do you want to stop hardware entirely and pursue licensing only, or keep the kiosk as a branded event rental?
