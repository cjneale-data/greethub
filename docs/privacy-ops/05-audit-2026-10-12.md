## Part B. Recurring audit checklist (quarterly, and before launch)

Record pass, fail or "not verifiable" with the date. Evidence is ids and counts, never content.

1. Consent: upload refused without boxes A and B (test `consent.test.ts`); wording hash changes when the text changes; every job input carries `consentId`.
2. Purge: 30 of 30 daily cron runs returned 200 with `verify.overdue = 0` and `verify.orphanFiles = 0` (02 s2).
3. Purge sample: five purged and five deleted projects checked (02 s3).
4. Requests: every request in the log closed within 30 days; vendor steps ticked (01 s6).
5. Export: run a test account's export and confirm it contains `books[].consentRecords` and no storage paths.
6. Logs: search one week of runtime logs for an email address pattern, `data:image`, and a test child's first name. Expect zero hits.
7. Pages: no third-party script on creator, proof, cart, checkout or account pages (req 44).
8. Vendors: 04 table current; sub-processor notices read; DMCA renewal date in the calendar.
9. Policy text matches code: re-run Part A for any row that changed.
10. Access: who can reach production data (data/06 s5) reviewed.
