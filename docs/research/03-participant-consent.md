# Participant consent form (draft)

**Draft for the owner's attorney to review before first use. This is not legal advice.** Questions for the attorney are at the end. Plain language, read aloud at the start of the session and signed (e-signature is fine) before recording starts. Version: research-consent-v0.1, 2026-10-12.

---

## Helping us test GreetHub

Thank you for helping. Here's what this session is and what happens to what you share.

**What we're testing.** A website for making personalized cards and picture books with AI-illustrated art. We're testing the website, not you. There are no wrong answers.

**What you'll do.** Use the site while you say out loud what you're thinking, then answer a few questions. It takes about 45 to 60 minutes.

**Please don't use real photos of children.** We'll give you photos of made-up people to use. Please don't upload photos of your own children or anyone else's. Everything you enter is a test. Payment uses a test card, and nothing is charged or printed.

**Recording.** With your permission we'll record your screen and voice. Your camera is optional; you can leave it off. If you open your own email or accounts by mistake, we'll pause the recording.

**Who sees it.** The GreetHub team. Notes use a number (like "P3"), not your name. We may show short clips inside the team with your name removed. We won't post anything publicly, use it in ads, or use it to train AI.

**How long we keep it.** Recordings are deleted 90 days after the session. Notes without your name are kept to help us improve the site.

**Your choices.** Taking part is up to you. You can skip any question, take a break, or stop at any time and still get your thank-you gift. You can ask us to delete your recording at any time before the 90 days by emailing {research contact}.
