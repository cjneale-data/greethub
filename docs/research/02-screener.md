# Recruiting screener

For usability sessions (`01`) and assumption interviews (`06`). Adults 18+ in the U.S. only. About 3 minutes to answer. The recruiter (owner or a panel) runs it; the answers stay with the recruiter and only the participant id and profile line come to us.

## 1. Questions

Intro shown to the respondent: "We're looking for people to try a new website for making personalized cards and picture books. It's a paid session of about 45 to 60 minutes by video. A few questions first to see if it's a fit."

1. **Are you 18 or older?** Yes / No. *No: end, thank them.*
2. **Where do you live?** U.S. state (list) / Outside the U.S. *Outside the U.S.: end.*
3. **In the last 12 months, have you bought any of these?** (select all) A personalized gift from Etsy or a similar shop / A photo book or photo gift / A personalized or custom greeting card / A personalized children's book / None of these. *None: end.*
4. **Is there a child aged 0 to 8 in your life you buy gifts for?** Yes, my child / Yes, my grandchild / Yes, a niece, nephew or friend's child / No. *Book sessions need a Yes; card-only slot (profile 4) accepts No.*
5. **Do you or anyone in your household work in web design, software, marketing, market research, or for a greeting card or photo gift company?** Yes / No. *Yes: end.*
6. **Which device do you use most to shop online?** iPhone / Android phone / iPad or other tablet / Laptop or desktop.
7. **Do you use any of these when you use a phone or computer?** (optional, select all) Screen reader / Screen magnifier or large text / Voice control / Keyboard only or a switch / None / Prefer not to say. *Used only to fill profile 5. Never required.*
8. **How do you feel about AI tools that make pictures?** I use them / I'm curious / I'm wary / I avoid them. *Aim for a mix; at least one "wary".*
9. **How comfortable are you talking out loud while you use a website, with your screen shared?** 1 (not at all) to 5 (very). *1 or 2: end.*
10. **Which of these times work for you?** (slots)

Do not ask for names, ages or photos of any child. Do not ask about health.

| Profile | Q4 | Q6 | Other |

| P-a Mother 30 to 40 | my child | iPhone | |

| P-c Grandparent 60+ | my grandchild | tablet or laptop | self-rated low or medium tech confidence (ask at booking) |
| P-d Card sender, no children of their own | niece/nephew/friend or No | any | sends cards for occasions |
| P-e Assistive technology user | any Yes | any | Q7 not "None"; higher incentive |

At least one participant wary of AI (Q8), at least two outside big cities, and a spread of ages and backgrounds. Exclude anyone who knows the team.

## 3. Research opt-in for customers (after launch)

Wording for a separate, unticked checkbox on the order confirmation page or in Account (RES-4). Not on the consent modal, not combined with marketing email.

> "I'm happy for GreetHub to email me now and then about helping improve the site, for example a short paid video chat. No more than twice a year. I can say no any time."

Store: account id, time, wording version, withdrawn time. Recruit post-launch sessions only from people who ticked it.
