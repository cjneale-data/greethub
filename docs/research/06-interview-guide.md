# Assumption interviews: 30 minutes, no product needed

Purpose: test assumptions A2 (photo trust), A3 (price) and A8 (AI-written story) from the README before the build is ready. Five to eight adults from the screener (`02`), same consent form (`03`, without the self-photo box). Run by video. One moderator; a second person takes notes. Incentive: half of the usability rate is a fair start (owner sets it).

Rules: ask about past behaviour before opinions; never pitch; never show competitor brands by name in materials; no photos of children on screen. Notes with P-ids only.

## 1. Warm-up (3 min)
- "Tell me about the last time you gave a child a gift that was made just for them." Who for, what, where bought, how much, how it went.

## 2. The last personalized gift (8 min)
- "Walk me through how you chose it." Where did you look? What made you pick that one?
- "What did you worry about before you ordered?" (Probe: arriving on time, looking right, price, photos.)
- "When it arrived, what happened?" How did the child react? Would you do it again?
- If they have made a photo book: "How did you feel about uploading photos?" Who do you think sees them?

## 3. Photos and AI (8 min)
- "Some sites draw a picture book from photos of a child using AI. What's your first reaction?" Let them talk.
- "What would you want to know before you uploaded your child's or grandchild's photo?" Write their list in their words.
- "If a site said it deletes the photos after 90 days and never uses them to train AI, would that change anything? What would make you believe it?"
- "How would you feel if the story text was written by AI from your notes, rather than by you?" Then: "And if you could edit every word before it prints?"
- Show one plain sentence on screen: "AI-illustrated. Story from your details." Ask: "What does this tell you? Would you want this on the cover?"

## 4. Price (6 min)
Describe without brand: "A hardcover picture book, 8.5 by 8.5 inches, 24 pages, with the child drawn as the main character, from your photos and story ideas. You approve the character and every page before it prints."
- "At what price would this seem so cheap you'd doubt the quality?"
- "At what price would it start to seem expensive but still worth considering?"
- "At what price would it be too expensive to consider?"
- Repeat for "a folded 5 by 7 card with original art, mailed for you to the person".
- "Would you pay extra for 32 pages instead of 24? How much?"
Record the numbers as given. Five to eight people is a direction, not a price point; the CFO should treat it as such.

## 5. Wrap-up (5 min)
- "If you could change one thing about how these gifts are made or sold, what would it be?"
- "Is there anything that would stop you from trying a new site for this?"
- "May we contact you again for a usability test later?" (Only if they ticked research contact in the screener.)

## 6. Analysis
One row per participant in `07-findings-template.md` (interview section). Code answers to the assumption ids. Report each assumption as supported / mixed / contradicted / no signal, with quotes. Never report a percentage on fewer than 10 people; report counts ("4 of 6").
