# docs/support

Support agent's working folder.

- `macros.md`: corrections to the canned replies in `docs/cx/02-support-model.md` section 10 where they promise actions the product can't do yet, the internal block every draft must carry, and holiday-season replies (H1 to H6).
- `patterns.md`: contact reasons and defects, for CX and engineering. Counts and order ids only, no customer content.

Drafts are never sent by the agent. A human sends every reply and does every refund, cancel and reprint until the admin actions in docs/cx/02 s11 exist.
