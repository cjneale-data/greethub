## 1. Positioning

**One line:** Your photos, in a designed card, checked before a single one prints.

**Why us, in order**
1. **Checked before it prints.** Read-back panel, name and year checks, weekday-matches-date, DPI warnings, a named approval ("Approve and print 50 cards"). A typo on 100 cards is the fear; we answer it.
2. **One design, paper and page.** Save the dates and invitations become an event page with RSVP from the same design.
3. **Plain prices, no promo maze.** One price ladder, envelopes included. We don't run 50%-off culture (CFO).
4. **Your hand on it.** Handwriting styles for the sign-off; your own handwriting with Plus once memberships open.
5. **Optional illustrated art** in our own styles (Phase 2+), something photo-only shops can't offer.

**Against (internal only; never name competitors in ads):**

| | Their strength | Our angle |
|---|---|---|
| Minted | Artist-designed templates, foil, free addressing; about $2.28 a card at 50 (SOURCED, plan sources) | Below on price ($1.79 at 50, proposal), and we check the words, not just the layout. Match free addressing in Phase 2, or we lose the comparison |
| Shutterfly | Range and reach; list prices with near-constant deep promos (not verified) | One honest price; no "wait for the sale" |
| Paperless Post | Digital invitations, per-guest premium sends about $0.22 to $0.30 (SOURCED, plan sources) | Event Plus $14.99 per event flat (proposal); free page up to 75 guests; paper and digital from one design |

Do not claim "better than", "cheapest" or "best quality" against anyone until market/03 and a physical proof exist.

## 4. Holiday 2026 campaign (only if Oct 24 is go)

PM scope: invite-only, 30-order cap, ship to me, 25/50/100, order-by Dec 5, no paid ads, SEO or creators. So this is a quiet owned-channel test, not a campaign.

- **Audience:** soft-launch allowlist and waitlist sign-ups only.
- **Message:** "Your year, on one card." Proof points 1 and 3 only. Price line: "From $1.59 a card, envelopes included" (CFO proposal, owner to approve before use).
- **Asset:** one email, one in-app banner on the card creator, one help article (Support).
- **Goal:** learning, not revenue: proof-to-order rate, contacts per order, delivery by Dec 23 (PM guardrails).
- **If no-go:** nothing ships or is teased. A waitlist line on the cards page, "Holiday photo cards arrive in October 2027. Want a note when they do?", owner to approve.

## 6. Email timing (customers who opted in to marketing only)

Guest lists and address books are **never** used for marketing (plan section 7). RSVP reminders are host-sent, transactional, and carry no promotion.

| Product | Emails |
|---|---|
| Holiday 2026 (if go) | Nov 20 open; Dec 1 "Order by Dec 5 for Christmas"; Dec 4 "Tomorrow is the last day"; Dec 5 a.m. "Last day". Draft left 24 h: one reminder. After Dec 5: one New Year card note |
| Holiday 2027 | Oct 1 open; Nov 1; Nov 15 (peak lead time); then order-by minus 5, minus 1, day of. Cut-off dates read from config, never typed |
| Save the dates | If the couple gave a wedding date: at about 8 months out "Time for save the dates", at 6 months a last nudge (12 and 9 months if they tick destination) |
| Invitations | About 14 weeks before the wedding "Time to order invitations" (mail 6 to 8 weeks out plus print and ship); RSVP-by reminder to the host only |

Every order-by email states the arrival window ("Order by Dec 5 to arrive by Dec 22"), matching the creator screen (CX).

## 7. Membership alongside

- **2026:** memberships are off in production and there are no member batch discounts (PM). Say nothing about members on photo-card pages.
- **2027 (proposal, CFO section 8):** Plus 10% and Card Club 15% off batch cards, Event Plus included, own handwriting on sign-offs and return addresses, saved address lists.
- **How we sell it:** one line under the price, not a banner: "With Plus, your own handwriting signs every card, and batch orders are 10% off." On invitation pages: "Event Plus is included with Plus."
- **Honesty check:** 10% off 50 holiday cards saves about $9; Plus is $35.99 a year. The discount alone does not pay for Plus, so never say "pays for itself". Lead with handwriting, as the membership page does.
