# Odd hands, teeth, or other odd details

*Status: draft.*

AI art sometimes gets small things wrong: an extra finger, a blurry hand, teeth that look strange, a stray object in the background. We check each page automatically, but we can't catch everything. You can.

## What to do on the proof

1. Open the page and zoom in.
2. Choose **Redraw this page**. Add a short note ("hands open, no extra objects").
3. Look at the new version. Keep it, or redraw again while you have redraws left.

Each picture book comes with 3 page redraws. A redraw that fails never uses one up. Before any pages are drawn, you also get 2 free redos of the character sheet. Cards don't have redraws once you're signed in; you can try a fresh preview instead.

## Tips

- Say what you want, not what you don't ("hands in pockets" works better than "no weird hands").
- Simple scenes draw more reliably than crowded ones. You can edit the story text to simplify a page.
- Pages with many people and small details are the hardest.

## Out of redraws?

Email {support_email} with your project name and the page number. A person will look at it. If it's clearly a drawing error, we'll work out a fix with you at no cost. This is our call, not a rule, and we're generous about it.

## Important

Please look at every page before you approve. Details visible in an approved proof aren't covered by a refund, but we still want to help. Ask us.
