# Change or cancel an order

*Status: draft. The Cancel button does not exist in the app yet (CX-11); until it does, support cancels by hand.*

## Cancel

Email {support_email} with your order number, or use **Ask us about this order** on the order page. Orders go to our print shop soon after you pay, so write as soon as you can. If printing hasn't started, we cancel it and refund you in full to your original payment method within 7 business days.

After printing starts we can't cancel, because it's already being made for you. Write to us anyway and we'll see what we can do.

## Change the address

Email {support_email} with your order number and the new address as soon as you can. If it hasn't shipped, we'll change it. If it has, we'll ask the carrier to redirect it, which doesn't always work. A wrong address that you gave us isn't covered by a reprint.

## Change the book or card

Printed items can't be edited after payment. If printing hasn't started, we may be able to cancel for a refund so you can make a corrected version. Write straight away.

## Change the delivery date

If it hasn't shipped, tell us. We'll say what's possible.

## Add another item

Place a second order. Both can go to the same address.
