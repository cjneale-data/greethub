# Delete your photos and your account

*Status: draft.*

You can remove your data yourself, and we'll do it for you if you'd rather email.

## Delete only the photos

1. Open your book from Account, Drafts.
2. Go to the Photos step.
3. Choose **Withdraw consent and delete photos**. Every photo you added to this book is deleted. Pages already drawn stay.

## Delete everything

1. Go to **Account, Delete account**.
2. Read where your data lives and what we keep (order records for tax, with your name and address removed after delivery).
3. Type DELETE, choose **Delete my account**, then confirm from the email we send you.

Orders already printing will still be printed and shipped.

## Get a copy first

On the same page, choose **Download my data**. You get a file with your profile, addresses, drafts, saved people and orders.

## Prefer to ask us?

Email {privacy_email} from your account email. We confirm within 3 business days and finish within 30 days. We'll email you when it's done.

## What we can't remove

Payment records held by Stripe, and order and tax records we have to keep by law. Your name and address are removed from our copy of the order after delivery.
