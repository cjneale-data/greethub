# Photos that work best

*Status: draft.*

Good photos make characters that look like your people. Here is what helps.

- **One clear face per photo.** Looking at the camera or close to it. A face the size of a coin or bigger.
- **Good light.** Daylight beats a dark room. Avoid heavy shadows across the face.
- **No sunglasses, masks or big hats** in the photo you use for the likeness.
- **Recent.** Children change fast; use a photo from the last few months.
- **Two or three photos help**: one straight on, one smiling, one from the side.
- **Size.** JPG, PNG or HEIC, at least 600 pixels on the short side. A normal phone photo is more than enough.
- **Only your photos, or photos you have permission to use.** For a child, you need to be the parent or guardian, or have the parent's written permission.
