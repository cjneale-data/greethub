# Refunds and reprints

*Status: draft that mirrors the Refund and Reprint Policy draft (docs/legal/05). Values in {braces} wait for the attorney and owner.*

Every card and book is made just for you, so we check it with you before it prints. Here's how we make it right.

## Before you pay

Preview, edit and redraw within your allowance. Nothing prints until you approve the proof.

## Cancel before printing starts

Full refund. Cancel from your order page until the status says "In production". Refunds go back to your card within 7 business days.

## We'll reprint or refund, your choice, if

- The print is damaged, defective or misprinted (wrong colors, cropped, torn or missing pages).
- It was damaged on the way to you.
- It doesn't match the proof you approved.
- It's lost in the mail ({lost_days} days after the estimated delivery).

Email {support_email} within {claim_days} days of delivery with your order number and a photo. You don't need to send the item back unless we ask.

## What isn't covered

- A change of mind after you approved the proof.
- Likeness or style that was visible in the approved proof.
- A wrong address you gave us. Tell us fast; if the carrier can still redirect it, we'll help.
- Damage after delivery.

## Goodwill

If an approved proof just isn't what you hoped, tell us. We may offer extra redraws or a discount on a new version. That's our choice, not a right, and we'd rather you were happy.

## Digital items

Refunded only if the file is faulty or we couldn't deliver it.

## Gifts

The person who paid is the customer. The recipient can email us and we'll sort it out with the buyer.
