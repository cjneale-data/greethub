# How we keep your photos private

*Status: draft. Every sentence must match the live product and the attorney-approved Privacy Policy before this is published. Do not publish a "we do not train on your photos" sentence unless the owner has confirmed the vendor settings (docs/legal/06 req 14).*

Photos of your family are the most personal thing you'll give us. Here is exactly what happens to them.

## What we do

- Your photos are stored privately. Only you and the systems that make your book can reach them.
- We send them to AI image and language services (named in the box you tick before uploading) to draw your characters. Our printers only receive finished artwork, never your original photos.
- We do not identify anyone by their face.
- We do not use your photos for advertising. If you tick the optional box saying we may show your finished pages as an example, we may. That box is off unless you turn it on.
- **We delete your photos 90 days after you add them**, unless you choose to keep a character for reuse.

## What you can do

- **Withdraw consent** on the Photos step. That deletes every photo you added to that book. Pages already drawn stay.
- **Download your data** from Account, Delete account, "Download my data".
- **Delete everything**: [Delete your photos and your account](05-delete-photos-and-account.md).
- **Remove a photo of you or your child** that someone else uploaded: use the [Report content form](/report). We aim to answer within 48 hours.

## Photos of children

You must be the parent or legal guardian, or have written permission from them. We ask you to confirm before every upload. GreetHub accounts are for adults only.
