# A name is spelled wrong

*Status: draft.*

## Before you pay

Go back to the step with the name and fix it, then redraw any page that shows it. Look at every page again.

## After you pay, before printing starts

Email {support_email} right away with your order number and the correct spelling. If printing hasn't started we'll fix it and send you a new proof to approve. Printing starts quickly, so please write the same day.

## After it has printed

If the name was wrong in the proof you approved, our policy doesn't cover a free reprint, because it was visible and approved. But we know how it feels to find a misspelled name in a gift. Write to us anyway. We may offer a discount on a corrected copy. That's our choice, and we'll be fair. If we introduced the error (the print doesn't match the proof), we'll reprint or refund, your choice.

## Avoiding it

The proof screen has a spelling check that lists every name you gave us. Look at it, and look at the cover.
