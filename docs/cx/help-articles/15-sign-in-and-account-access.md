# Signing in and account access

*Status: draft.*

GreetHub has no passwords. You sign in with a link we email you, or with Google.

## I didn't get my sign-in link

1. Check spam and promotions folders. The email comes from {from_email}.
2. Check the address you typed. Typos are the most common reason.
3. Wait a minute and choose **Resend**. You can ask again after 30 seconds.
4. The link works for 15 minutes and once only. Ask for a new one if it expired.

## The link says it expired

Go back to Sign in and request a new link. Use the newest email.

## I signed up with Google but want a link (or the other way round)

Both work with the same email address, so you'll see the same drafts and orders.

## I changed my email address

Email {support_email} from your old address if you can, with your new address. We'll verify it's you before we change anything.

## I can't get into my email

Write to {support_email} from any address and tell us your name, your last order number and the first part of your shipping address. We'll help you recover access carefully, because your orders and photos are private.

## Who can have an account?

Adults, 18 and over. If you're under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to make the account.

## Sign out on a shared computer

Account, Sign out. Do this on any computer that isn't yours.
