# My payment was declined

*Status: draft.*

Your card was declined. Nothing has been charged and your cart and proof are safe.

## Try this

1. Check the card number, expiry date, security code and billing ZIP.
2. Try another card, or a digital wallet if one is offered.
3. Your bank may have paused it to check. A quick call or a tap in your bank's app often clears it.
4. Try again in a few minutes.

## I see a charge but got no confirmation

Wait five minutes, then check **Account, Orders**. A pending hold from a declined payment drops off your statement on its own, usually within a few days. If you see a charge and no order, email {support_email} with the last four digits of your card.

## Which payments do you accept?

Major credit and debit cards through Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.

## Still declined?

Email {support_email}. We can't see why your bank said no, but we can check your order is okay.
