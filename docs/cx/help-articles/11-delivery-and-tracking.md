# Delivery times and tracking

*Status: draft. Delivery ranges must come from live printer data (docs/legal/06 req 36). Do not publish fixed day counts until verified.*

We print each card or book to order, then mail it. Your estimate is shown in the cart, at checkout, in your confirmation email and on your order page. It adds printing time and delivery time.

## Where is my order?

1. Go to **Account, Orders** and open your order.
2. The steps are Paid, Preparing, Printing, Shipped, Delivered.
3. Once it ships, **Track package** takes you to the carrier.
4. We also email you when it ships.

## It hasn't come yet

- Check the estimate on your order page first.
- Tracking sometimes lags a day after pickup.
- If it's past the estimate, email {support_email} with your order number. We'll chase it. If it doesn't arrive within {lost_days} days after the estimate, we'll reprint or refund, your choice.

## If we're going to be late

We'll tell you the new date. You can wait, or cancel for a full refund.

## Need it by a date?

At checkout choose **By a date**. If we can't make that date we'll say so before you pay.
