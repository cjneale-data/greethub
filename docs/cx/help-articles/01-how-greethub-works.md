# How GreetHub works

*Help center. Status: draft. Replace {tokens} with launch values. Policy numbers follow the Refund and Reprint Policy once the attorney signs off.*

You tell us who it's for and what you'd say. We draw it, you check it, we print it and mail it.

## A card in four steps

1. **Tell us.** Chat with the Greet Guide or fill in the short form. Who it's for, the occasion, how it should feel, what the picture should show, what to say inside.
2. **See the preview.** Your first card is free to preview without an account. It has a watermark and it is low resolution, so you can judge the picture and the words.
3. **Make an account to keep going.** It's free. Your card comes with you. Accounts unlock full-size proofs, re-rolls each month, saved drafts and saved people.
4. **Approve it, pay, and we mail it.** Nothing is charged until you've looked at the proof and said yes.

## A picture book in six steps

Idea, photos, story, characters, pages, proof. Books take a little longer: see [Making a picture book](02-making-a-picture-book.md).

## Who draws the pictures?

An AI draws them. The words are yours; the Guide can help you write them. We say so on every page that shows art, because you should know. And if an order goes wrong, a person helps: see [Refunds and reprints](13-refunds-and-reprints.md).

## Who can use GreetHub?

Adults, 18 and over, in the United States. We ship to U.S. addresses for now.
