# Checking your proof

*Status: draft.*

The proof is what we print. Nothing prints until you approve it. Take five minutes; a printed book can't be changed.

## What to check

1. **Every name.** Spelling of each person, pet and place, on every page. The names come from what you typed.
2. **The title and dedication.** You can edit both on the proof screen.
3. **The words.** Read them aloud. Fix anything on the Story step before you draw, or tell us now.
4. **Each picture.** Faces, hands, anything odd in the background. Zoom in.
5. **The cover.** Title, spelling, who it's for.
6. **The delivery estimate and the address** at checkout.

## Redraws and edits

- **Redraw a page** from the proof (uses your monthly allowance).
- **Edit text** by going back a step.

## When you approve

You'll tick that you've reviewed it and that you understand it's made just for you. Then we make your print files and you can add it to the cart.

## Spotted something after you approved?

Email {support_email} with the order number straight away. If it hasn't started printing, we can often fix it. See [Change or cancel an order](14-change-or-cancel-an-order.md).
