# Checking your proof

*Status: draft.*

The proof is what we print. Nothing prints until you approve it. Take five minutes; a printed book can't be changed.

## What to check

1. **Every name.** Spelling of each person, pet and place, on every page. The names come from what you typed.
2. **The title.** You can edit it on the proof screen.
3. **The words.** Read them aloud. Fix anything on the Story step before you draw, or tell us now.
4. **Each picture.** Faces, hands, anything odd in the background. Zoom in.
5. **The cover.** Title, spelling, who it's for.
6. **The delivery estimate and the address** at checkout.

## Redraws and edits

- **Redraw a page** from the proof. Each book has 3 redraws, and the button shows how many are left. A redraw that fails doesn't use one up.
- **Edit text** by going back a step.

## When you approve

You'll tick "I've looked at every page and I'm happy to print it." Then we make your print files and you can add it to the cart. Printed books can't be changed after you pay.

## Spotted something after you approved?

Email {support_email} with the order number straight away. Orders go to our print shop soon after you pay, so the first hour or two matters. If printing hasn't started, we can often fix it. See [Change or cancel an order](14-change-or-cancel-an-order.md).
