# It doesn't look like us

*Status: draft.*

AI likeness is close, never exact. Here's how to get closer.

## Before the pages are drawn (best time)

On the **Characters** step you see a sheet for each person. If it's off:
1. Mark that person **Something's off**.
2. Say what to change in a few words ("longer curly hair, round glasses").
3. Choose **Redo the sheet**. You get {free_redos} free redos.

A better photo helps more than a better description: see [Photos that work best](03-photos-that-work.md).

## After the pages are drawn

You can redraw single pages from the proof. Each book has 3 page redraws, and the character sheet gets 2 free redos before any pages are drawn. A redraw uses the approved character sheet, so the face won't change much. If the sheet itself was off, tell us at {support_email} before you approve.

## After you approve

Once you've approved a proof and it has printed, differences in likeness that were visible in the proof aren't covered by our refund policy. If the print truly doesn't match what you approved, email us with a photo of the page and your order number and we'll look. See [Refunds and reprints](13-refunds-and-reprints.md).

## Can't get it right?

Write to {support_email}. A person will help you.
